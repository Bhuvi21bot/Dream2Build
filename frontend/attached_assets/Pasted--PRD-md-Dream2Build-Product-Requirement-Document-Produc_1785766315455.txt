# Dream2Build - Product Requirement Document

## Product Vision

Dream2Build is an AI-powered platform that helps users design, plan, optimize, estimate, visualize, and build homes using artificial intelligence.

Instead of manually creating house plans, users simply describe their requirements, and Dream2Build generates complete architectural layouts, cost estimates, material requirements, climate analysis, furniture recommendations, and construction reports.

---

# Problem Statement

Building a house is expensive, time-consuming, and requires multiple professionals.

Users often struggle with

* Finding architects
* Understanding construction cost
* Planning room layouts
* Ventilation
* Sunlight
* Furniture placement
* Material estimation
* Construction planning

Dream2Build solves all of these using AI.
